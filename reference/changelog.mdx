<Update label="November 2026" description="Artwork after checkout">
  - [Artwork after checkout](/configuration/artwork-after-checkout): customers can pay first and upload their artwork later, on your own branded upload page at `upload.filecheck.io/your-store`. Each file is checked against its product's Workflow and the proof is approved on the page.
  - Choose per product, or for the whole store: **At checkout** (the default), **Optional, or after checkout**, or **After checkout only**.
  - The upload page uses your logo, colour and message, and follows each customer's language. Customers sign in with their order number and email, or open a signed link.
  - Links reach customers through Shopify's order confirmation email, the WooCommerce plugin (1.0.7) and the PrestaShop module (1.1.0), and optional **Upload your artwork** and **Proof ready** emails from Filecheck.
  - **Orders → Awaiting artwork** lists orders still waiting, with copy link and reminder.
  - API: [`POST /orders/{id}`](/api-reference/orders/capture-an-order) takes `awaitArtwork`, `workflowId` and `orderName`; new [`GET /orders/{id}/upload-link`](/api-reference/orders/get-an-order-upload-link). The Element gains `artworkTiming`.
</Update>

<Update label="September 2026" description="3D Proofs">
  - [3D Proofs](/concepts/3d-proofs): customers see and approve their artwork on the product (a 3D model, a folded leaflet, a booklet or a flat sheet) instead of on flat pages.
  - [3D Scenes](/configuration/3d-scenes) in the Library, built in the Scene Studio, with ready-made Store scenes for leaflets, booklets and sheets.
  - A Workflow's Soft-proofing step chooses between flat pages and a 3D Scene.
  - Scenes can [supply sides and covers](/configuration/3d-scenes#sides-and-covers-you-supply) as a colour or an image, such as a single-sided business card with a green back, or a booklet's back cover.
  - Flat sheets and folded leaflets can show [painted edges](/configuration/3d-scenes#painted-edges) in a colour.
  - **3D Proof in Product Gallery** (Settings → Element) shows the proof in place of the product photos. `Filecheck.mount` gains `galleryProof` and `gallerySelector`, and the Element gains a `scene` event.
</Update>
