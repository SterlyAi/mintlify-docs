<Update label="September 2026" description="3D Proofs">
  - [3D Proofs](/concepts/3d-proofs): customers see and approve their artwork on the product (a 3D model, a folded leaflet, a booklet or a flat sheet) instead of on flat pages.
  - [3D Scenes](/configuration/3d-scenes) in the Library, built in the Scene Studio, with ready-made Store scenes for leaflets, booklets and sheets.
  - A Workflow's Soft-proofing step chooses between flat pages and a 3D Scene.
  - Scenes can [supply sides and covers](/configuration/3d-scenes#sides-and-covers-you-supply) as a colour or an image, such as a single-sided business card with a green back, or a booklet's back cover.
  - **3D Proof in Product Gallery** (Settings → Element) shows the proof in place of the product photos. `Filecheck.mount` gains `galleryProof` and `gallerySelector`, and the Element gains a `scene` event.
</Update>
